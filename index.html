Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning permissions, guarantees, timescales, and costs.

In most London properties, no! Most rear dormer, hip-to-gable, and rooflight conversions fall under Permitted Development (PD) rights (up to 40m³ for terraced homes or 50m³ for semi-detached). If your home is in a Conservation Area or is a flat, full planning permission is required. Our architectural team manages all Certificate of Lawfulness applications on your behalf.
Building regulations require a minimum head height of 2.0 to 2.2 metres from the existing ceiling joists to the ridge timber. If your current attic height is borderline (e.g. 1.9m – 2.1m), our structural engineers can lower the first-floor ceilings or employ ultra-slim profile steel beams to achieve full building control sign-off.
On-site construction typically takes 6 to 8 weeks for a standard dormer or 8 to 10 weeks for an expansive L-shaped mansard. Approximately 75% of the heavy structural work is executed through external scaffolding, meaning minimal disruption to your rooms below until staircase fitting.
